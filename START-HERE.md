# Start here

**[Deutsch starten](learning/start-de.md)**

For a first taste, try the [notebook calculator](exercises/verification-lab/README.md), in English or German. Allow about five minutes for a first try; fixing it can take longer. No account needed. You can use a browser or paper.

Want to keep going? Choose a path below. You can stop after any lesson and come back another day; the times are only estimates.

## Follow your chosen path

Follow the list on this page. A lesson's **Next** link follows the full course order and may take you somewhere else.

If a lesson assumes something you have not tried, use its earlier lesson first. Before the verification lesson, read [Model, context, agent](tracks/04-the-map/01-model-context-agent.md) if those words are new to you.

Want to check what you remember? Ask your agent for a new example to try, or use a [practice question](learning/checkpoints.md). Try it yourself before asking for the answer.

When you stop, jot down where you got to and what you want to try next. There is an optional [notes template](learning/progress-template.md); keep your notes private. Next time, try explaining the last step before moving on.

## How to use a unit

Open a lesson and go to **Do it** for the steps. Its opening table tells you what you need first and roughly how long it takes. **Done when** helps you check what you have tried before moving on.

Text in grey boxes is a prompt or command to copy. Tool instructions include a check date and a source link; if your screen looks different, look at the vendor's current help page.

## When you are stuck

- **A word is unfamiliar:** look it up in the [glossary](reference/glossary.md).
- **An exercise is unclear:** ask your agent or someone learning with you. A short error message can help; remove names, keys and private paths before sharing it. Check screenshots for those too.
- **The same fix has failed twice:** save your current attempt. Note what you wanted, what last worked, the error you see, what you tried and one thing to check next. Use that note to start a fresh chat. [More help](tracks/05-verify-and-loop/02-five-failure-patterns.md).
- **You cannot install or sign in:** use the vendor help link in the [setup guides](README.md#do-it-yourself-accounts-licences-hosting). While access is blocked, you can still do the [notebook exercise](exercises/verification-lab/README.md) on paper.

A trainer can help with an exercise. Accounts and hosting are arranged by you or your organisation; the setup guides do not provide an account or support service.
